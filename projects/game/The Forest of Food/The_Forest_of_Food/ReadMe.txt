Game Name:The forest of Food

GAM150S20KR
High Concept:The Forest of food is 3rd person Top - view adventure game. 
The player can choose the favorite seasonal food. 
And go to the forest of food to make seasonal food.
 Escaping the forest of food, the player finds the ingredient of food on the forest of food. 
Player kills the pig to get Pork, gets the mushroom and steals the crops from farm. 
Then Player makes seasonal food out of collected  materials. Player should satisfy the fairy of the forest to escape.
Installation:
In installation folder, execute 'The forest of Food_Setup.exe'. And Run it.
How to play:
You should find the materials of seasonal food for escaping the forest of food. Kill the Animal and pick up the items. 
And cooking the seasonal food with your items that you picked on the map! 
Controls:
Arrow keys - move
Space key - attack
Control key - interact 
Shift key - Running
Z key - inventory
Cheat Codes:
In Map stage, Spring('U' key) Summer('I'key) Fall('O'key) Winter('P'key).
In cooking stage, Enter(Skip the Cooking stage)
